# Models and weights

Claude and GPT have published hardware prototypes. To run a model yourself, try SchGen for schematics, Micius-Flash for engineering tasks, or PCBWorld for routing. [Results on public benchmarks](benchmarks.md)

## General-purpose models from major developers

- [Claude Fable 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-electronics-design) — **Three working prototypes.** Anthropic and Diode gave Claude short product briefs for a USB-C speakerphone, a voice-assistant hub, and a handheld virtual pet. Claude designed the boards, enclosures, and firmware. Diode engineers built and tested all three prototypes. Anthropic reports that they “worked without any fixes to their circuitry.” Antenna tuning, thermal testing, and certification remained open. Starting with Sonnet 4.5, the partners also trained Claude to generate reference circuits from chip datasheets and checked the results against electrical requirements. [Model announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) · [Training work with Diode](https://claude.com/resources/articles/making-claude-a-better-electrical-engineer)
- [GPT-6 Astra](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) — **KiCad layout and a handheld device.** OpenAI’s launch video shows Astra placing components and routing copper in KiCad from an existing schematic. In a later project, Astra selected parts and designed the board and enclosure for a handheld voice interface to Codex. Two team members worked on the project. OpenAI reports that a contract manufacturer built “a small batch of prototypes.” The design went through several reviews of tolerances and assembly details. OpenAI describes training for professional software environments. The PCB training data and method remain undisclosed. [Handheld prototype report](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-engineering-for-the-physical-world/)
- [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) — **Evidence for the later release.** The 6.2 announcement lists the BenchCAD 2.1 engineering benchmark. The KiCad video and handheld prototype used GPT-6 Astra. Record the model version and reasoning setting separately when reproducing a run. OpenAI defines Ultra as a reasoning mode that can distribute work to several agents. [Models and reasoning settings](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/models)
- [GLM-5.4](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4) — **Mechanical design and 3D demonstrations.** Z.ai showed the model building a six-axis arm in FreeCAD and animating a grasp in Blender. In a separate test, TOKRACE published a Blender handheld project. Its exploded view shows the shape of a board. The files contain 3D meshes and renders. Neither source provides a schematic, copper connections, or details of PCB-specific training. Z.ai opened GLM Coding Plan access on November 23 and announced plans to release weights. Its Hugging Face account listed no 5.4 weights on November 24. [Handheld project and script](https://www.tokrace.com/en/guides/glm-5-4#handheld) · [Z.ai model directory](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/models?search=GLM-5.4)

## Specialist models for PCB and engineering tasks

### Downloadable weights

- [Micius-Flash](https://huggingface.co/zhiman-ai/Micius-Flash) — **Zhiman’s 27B multimodal engineering model, with full weights.** Working from a blank schematic and fixed board outline, the model operated KiCad through Micius Harness. It produced a 5 V soft-latch circuit and a two-layer board with nine footprints, then checked the netlist and copper connectivity. The team describes semiconductor training and tool-use examples checked by execution. The 11 BF16 weight files total about 54.7 GB and carry a noncommercial research license. The KiCad example also requires the separate harness and tools. [Weight files](https://huggingface.co/zhiman-ai/Micius-Flash/tree/main) · [PCB case study](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10015v1#S6.p5) · [Tool replay and API](https://zhiman.cn/models/micius)
- [SchGen](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/SchGen) — **LoRA adapter for editable schematics.** An adapter adds learned parameters to a base model. Microsoft’s release uses `openai/gpt-oss-20b`. An example asks for a 3.3 V regulator based on the AP2112K. The model generates Python code, and the export tool produces a KiCad schematic. [Adapter configuration](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/SchGen/blob/main/adapter_config.json) · [Inference and export example](https://github.com/microsoft/SchGen)
- [AI KiCad PCB Architectural Planner](https://github.com/Ssbullock/pcb-layout-llm) — **Planning adapter for placement and routing.** The author fine-tuned Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct on examples from KiCad designs. The model reads a netlist and a summary of footprint geometry. It outputs two JSON plans for component grouping, power trace widths, and routing channels. A separate placer and router calculate positions and draw traces. The release includes training scripts and LoRA parameters. [Adapter files](https://huggingface.co/Simonssb/qwen2.5-coder-32b-pcb-grpo/tree/main)
- [PCBWorld](https://huggingface.co/LG-AI-Research/PCBWorld) — **Policies for step-by-step routing.** A policy reads the routing state and selects the next action for the KiCad engine. The D1 directory contains policies trained on regular grids. D2 contains policies trained on gridless synthetic boards. The authors specify research and educational use. [Checkpoint directories](https://huggingface.co/LG-AI-Research/PCBWorld/tree/main) · [Routing environment](https://github.com/LGAI-Research/PCBWorld)
- [CADET](https://huggingface.co/LG-AI-Research/CADET) — **Six routing checkpoints.** Compare `cadet_bc.pt` and `cadet.pt` on the same unrouted board. The first learns from demonstrated actions. The second adds reinforcement learning after that stage. Both issue actions through PCBWorld. KiCad can check the resulting board. The authors specify research and educational use. [Weight files](https://huggingface.co/LG-AI-Research/CADET/tree/main) · [Execution and checks](https://github.com/LGAI-Research/CADET)

### Hosted placement and routing service

- [Quilter](https://www.quilter.ai/product) — **Hosted placement and routing service.** Upload a schematic and a board file with its outline, mounting holes, and fixed components. The service returns editable candidate layouts. The team reports training its system on synthetic boards with physical constraints. Project Speedrun provides several revisions of two boards for a Linux computer. Compare an automatic layout with the production files after engineering cleanup. [Input requirements](https://docs.quilter.ai/get-started/quickstart) · [Example design files](https://www.quilter.ai/project-speedrun/download)

To train a placement policy from scratch, follow the RL_PCB example. To score an existing schematic, run the PCBSchemaGen pin checker. [Training and reproduction](training.md) · [PCBSchemaGen checking code](https://github.com/HZou9/PCBSchemaGen_v2/blob/main/framework/score_design.py)
