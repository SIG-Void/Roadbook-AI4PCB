## Contribute to the directory

Found a PCB paper, project, product, or tutorial to include? Send the link and one sentence about it. You can also add code or data, fix a broken link, or contribute a working Harbor environment.

## What we study

We document where engineers use large language models in PCB design. We compare public benchmarks with those tasks to find gaps. We use action traces and submitted designs to study where Chinese models succeed or need improvement. [Benchmarks and submissions](benchmarks.md#ale) · [Engineering notes](blog.md)

<h2 id="collaborate">Work with SIGVoid</h2>

- **Define PCB benchmark standards.** PCB specialists can propose engineering tasks, specify the files to submit, write scoring rules, and review model submissions.
- **Help large language models design better PCBs.** We study how models apply design constraints, compare alternatives, and check a board. Methods include supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL).
- **Commission PCB training data.** We can prepare task-specific examples, traces of engineers’ actions, and reference submissions with acceptance criteria.
